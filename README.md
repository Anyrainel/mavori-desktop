Mavori is an AI trading agent for individual investors. Start with a question, investigate the evidence, and bring what you learn into your portfolio and strategy decisions—all in one place.

Use the built-in agent to get started, or connect your own AI assistant through **MCP** (Model Context Protocol), available on paid plans. Both work with Mavori's research, portfolio, and strategy tools.

## From a question to a decision

### Research with evidence

Explore companies, market news, and the bigger economic picture. Ask follow-up questions, inspect sources, and understand where the available data leaves gaps.

> “What changed for NVDA this week, and what would challenge the bullish case?”

### Put your portfolio in the picture

Review holdings and exposure alongside your research. Use portfolio context to ask better questions about concentration, cash, and the risks you already carry.

> “Where am I most concentrated, and which positions share the same risks?”

<!-- Demo slot: assets/demos/portfolio.gif. See docs/demo-media.md. -->

### Test an idea before acting

Work with the agent to turn an idea into a strategy and examine its historical backtest. Review the assumptions, data coverage, and results before deciding what comes next. Strategy deployment is a separate step requiring explicit approval; availability depends on the strategy, account, and supported execution mode.

> “Help me build a moving-average strategy and inspect its drawdowns.”

<!-- Demo slot: assets/demos/strategy.gif. See docs/demo-media.md. -->

### Keep the work connected

Set alerts for conditions you care about, return to earlier conversations, and keep a journal of your reasoning. Your Mavori account connects your chats, portfolios, strategies, schedules, and alerts across desktop and web.

*Example prompts show ways to get started. Results depend on available data, connected accounts, and plan access. Backtests measure historical performance under stated assumptions; they do not predict future returns.*

### Get started

1. Download the app for your platform and open it.
2. Sign in or create a Mavori account.
3. Start a conversation with the built-in agent. No MCP setup is needed.

Mavori uses hosted services and requires an internet connection. Access to individual features depends on your plan and account permissions.

<details>
<summary>Installation notes</summary>

- **Windows:** The installer is currently unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may display a warning. Check that your download came from this repository's official release page before proceeding.
- **macOS:** Choose Apple silicon for an M-series Mac, or Intel for an Intel-based Mac. Open the DMG and drag Mavori into Applications.
- **Linux:** Make the AppImage executable in your file manager's permissions settings, then launch it.

</details>
